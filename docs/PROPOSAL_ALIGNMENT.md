## Presentation priorities

- Brand: **Farm-Capta Zimbabwe**.
- Tagline: **Our land, Our Agriculture, Our future.**
- Lead with credible farm evidence and better-informed financing decisions.
- Put farmer verification and the lender workspace ahead of investment discovery.
- Keep review badges specific. Verification does not guarantee ownership, profitability, repayment or approval.
- Use fictional records. Do not turn the proposal’s problem statement into unsupported live statistics or institutional claims.
- Use a restrained agricultural palette, clear typography, accessible controls, responsive layouts and lightweight field illustrations.

## Deliberate scope boundaries

This work is frontend only. Real authentication, secure file storage, server authorization, vendor KYC, email/SMS delivery and live financing are not implemented. Demo role selection is explicitly labelled; uploaded evidence stays in memory.

The proposal describes data-access fees, verification fees, potential logistics/carbon-related commissions, software licensing and a future regulated investment ecosystem. These are business-model directions, not instructions to add billing, carbon trading, subscriptions or financial transactions to the current proof of concept. Those features have not been added.

The proposal’s ask for funding, advisors and a controlled pilot remains presentation context. The interface makes no claim that those partnerships, approvals or a live pilot already exist.
